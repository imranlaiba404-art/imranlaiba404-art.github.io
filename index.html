Key Research Observations

  • NNPC demonstrated faster settling than PI control.
  • PI control showed more initial overshoot in the comparison.
  • NNPC produced a well-damped response.
  • NNPC eliminated steady-state error in the tested scenarios.

These findings are simulation-based and should not be interpreted as field or hardware deployment results.
